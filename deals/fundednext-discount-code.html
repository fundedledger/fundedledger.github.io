Watch out

  • Coupon-farm sites. Many "FundedNext code" pages list expired or invented codes, and they contradict each other on the percentage (7% to 30% for different codes). None is confirmed by FundedNext. I don't repeat them.
  • Refund terms changed in 2026. Stellar 1-Step fees are refunded with the third withdrawal for accounts bought or reset on or after 12 January 2026. Whether the refund equals the discounted price you paid isn't stated on the pages I read. Unknown.
  • Resets are excluded from the October Stellar offers, and benefits are lost if the account is reset.
  • US traders get less. The October offers are smaller for US traders, the 15% challenge reward isn't available to US-based clients, and a third-party review says CFDs aren't offered to US traders.
  • The payout speed claim conflicts with itself. FundedNext's homepage says "99.99% processed within 24 hours" and, on the same page, shows a 40-hour average processing time. Don't treat either as a promise.
  • It hasn't paid me. I trade FundedNext Futures and like the firm, but my payouts so far came from FTMO, Lucid Trading and Crypto Fund Trader. A cheap evaluation is only cheap if you get paid.
