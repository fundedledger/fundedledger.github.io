What a discount doesn't fix

  • The fee is the smaller number if you fail. A 40% cut on a $375 fee still costs you the rest. Most traders fail evaluations, and a cheaper attempt is only cheap if you pass.
  • The refund is not up front. FXIFY's FAQ says the purchase fee "gets reimbursed to you with your first payout on request" for 1, 2 and 3 phase plans. The homepage says "100% Refund" with no detail. The FAQ is the more specific source. A third party says Two Phase Pro fees are not reimbursed.
  • Resets. I found no reset rule or price on the FXIFY pages I read.
  • First payout. The FAQ lists a minimum of 50 USD and 5 minimum trading days. Trading days vary by program (sources differ). Later payouts are monthly, or every two weeks with a paid add-on.
  • Other costs. FXIFY says there are no hidden fees and that the purchase fee covers the challenge and verification. Commissions, spreads and swaps are in the FAQ.

Watch out

  • It hasn't paid me. I haven't traded FXIFY and have no payout experience with it. Everything here comes from public pages.
  • Payout complaints. Trustpilot shows 4.3/5 from 6,539 reviews (29 October 2026), but there are complaints about latency-arbitrage payout denials and KYC or payout delays. See the Trustpilot page and my FXIFY review.
  • Pro limits. Per the FXIFY blog and a third party, Two Phase Pro has no fee reimbursement and a $4,000 daily profit cap.
  • Not open to US residents, according to FXIFY's FAQ. Check before you buy.
  • Unclear licence claims. Third parties disagree on where FXIFY is registered, so I state none.
  • Sales end quickly. You may pay full price the day after a sale ends.
