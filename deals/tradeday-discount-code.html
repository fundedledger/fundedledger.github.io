What a discount doesn't fix

  • It's a subscription. You pay every month until you pass or cancel. TradeDay says subscription fees are non-refundable except where required by law. A slow evaluation costs more than the first card price. Source: how it works.
  • Resets cost money. The cards list a reset price equal to the monthly price shown. TradeDay's help center says you can get a free reset if you wait for your next subscription payment. Source: help center.
  • Funded-stage costs. There is no activation fee. Withdrawals by international wire cost $15 (US wire is free), and the funded live data fee is $156 a month per exchange. Sources: payout policy and fees FAQ.
  • The rules stay the same. A cheaper card doesn't change the pass rate. TradeDay's own footer puts it at 36% for January to June 2026.

Watch out

  • Quick Pay End of Day turns intraday once funded. The evaluation is end-of-day, but funded Quick Pay accounts trail intraday. I only buy end-of-day trailing on futures, because intraday trailing has cost me accounts before. That is my rule, not experience of TradeDay. Details in the review.
  • Fast Pass payout caps. On accounts opened on or after 26 July 2026 the per-cycle caps are lower than before. The review has the figures.
  • Unclear promo. A banner with no end date, no stated value and no conditions makes it hard to know if you are getting a real discount. Compare list and shown prices, as above.
  • Relaunched firm. TradeDay 2.0 relaunched on 29 May 2026, so older reviews describe different rules.
  • Simulated futures accounts. These are sim accounts, not real capital.
  • Coupon-farm pages. Third-party "TradeDay code" pages list different codes at different values. None is confirmed by TradeDay, so I don't repeat them.
  • It hasn't paid me. I haven't traded TradeDay, so I have no payout experience with it. A cheaper evaluation is only cheap if you get paid.
