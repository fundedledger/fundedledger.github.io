Which codes are real

  • Coupon-farm sites. Pages that list "30% off" codes for every firm often show expired or invented codes, and they contradict each other and the firms themselves. We publish only codes we hold and the firms' own public codes, labelled as theirs.
  • Codes we hold. LEDGER (FundedNext, Earn2Trade) and A39608C1 (E8 Markets). We can't tell you what they are worth; the checkout total is the only proof.
  • "No code of ours." Where we hold no code, we say so rather than borrow one from a third-party list.
  • A discount is not a reason to buy. A cheaper evaluation is only cheap if you pass and get paid. Most traders fail.

How to pay the least for a funded account

  1. Check for promotions firstMany firms run sales. Prices change, so the checkout price is the one that counts.
  2. Count the whole costActivation fees, monthly billing and add-ons can double the real price. See the math or use the cost calculator.
  3. Prefer refundable feesFTMO's 2-Step fee is reported to be refunded with your first reward. FundedNext's refund terms vary, so check FundedNext's rules.
  4. Don't buy cheap bad rulesA discount on intraday drawdown isn't a deal. It cost me accounts.
