How to pay the least for a funded account

  1. Never pay list priceMost firms run a sale most weeks. If there's no promotion today, there usually will be soon.
  2. Count the whole costActivation fees, monthly billing and add-ons can double the real price. See the math.
  3. Prefer refundable feesFTMO's and FundedNext's 2-Step fees come back with your first payout.
  4. Don't buy cheap bad rulesA discount on intraday drawdown isn't a deal. It cost me accounts.
