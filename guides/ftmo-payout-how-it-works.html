FTMO Payout: How It Works and How Long It Takes

You can request your first FTMO payout from day 14 after your first trade on the account. FTMO then reviews it within 1–2 business days and typically sends it 1–2 business days after approval. After the first one, FTMO's help centre states no fixed cycle.

FTMO has paid me four times in 2026, and each payout took about 2–3 days to process. Below is what FTMO says about the process, then what I've actually received.

The short answer

  • First payout: from the 14th day (or any later day) after the first trade on the account, with all open positions and pending orders closed.
  • Review: FTMO says it will review your account and notify you within 1–2 business days.
  • Sending: the reward is typically sent within 1–2 business days after the invoice is approved.
  • After the first reward: FTMO's help pages state no fixed payout cycle. Some aggregator sites say "every 14 days"; I couldn't confirm that on FTMO's site, so treat it as unconfirmed.
  • My own times: about 2–3 days to process each payout.

Checked 12 November 2026 against FTMO's help centre (sources at the bottom). FTMO can change its rules; confirm on FTMO's site before you buy.

How the first payout works

  1. Trade, then wait for day 14The clock starts at your first trade on that account. You can request on the 14th day or any day after it. The 14th day is the earliest date, not a schedule.
  2. Finish KYCFTMO's identity check (KYC/KYB) opens on 1-Step and 2-Step accounts once all evaluation objectives are met. On Instant accounts it's completed with the first reward request. No reward is processed until it's approved.
  3. Close everythingAll open positions and pending orders must be closed before you request.
  4. Request the rewardMinimum closed profit is $20 for bank wire and Visa Direct / Mastercard Send, and $50 for crypto. FTMO lists Visa Direct / Mastercard Send (up to $20,000), Skrill (up to $3,000), bank wire and crypto. It says it charges no additional commission on reward withdrawals.
  5. Review, then sendFTMO reviews within 1–2 business days, then typically sends within 1–2 business days after the invoice is approved.

Your share of the profit is 80% by default on the 2-Step, or 90% through the Scaling Plan or the Premium Programme. 1-Step and Instant accounts are 90% (split details last checked 4 November 2026).

What it costs you to wait

  • Day 14 is the floor. Even if you pass fast, you can't ask for a reward before the 14th day after your first trade.
  • Review plus sending adds time. By FTMO's own numbers that's 1–2 business days to review plus 1–2 to send, so roughly 2–4 business days from request to money on paper. Weekends and holidays stretch it.
  • KYC can hold things up. If you haven't completed it, nothing is processed.
  • The 2-Step fee refund waits for your first request. FTMO refunds the initial purchase fee once you request your first reward, to the same payment method. 1-Step and Instant fees aren't refundable.

What I've received

I've traded FTMO's 2-Step on 50K and 100K accounts and passed in roughly two weeks. Each payout since took about 2–3 days to process. That's one trader's experience, not a promise.

Watch outs

  • Closed book first. Open positions or pending orders mean the request isn't valid yet.
  • KYC before any reward. Do it as soon as FTMO opens it, so it isn't the thing you wait on.
  • Minimums. $20 closed profit for bank wire, Visa Direct and Mastercard Send; $50 for crypto.
  • The fee refund is 2-Step only and only with your first request. Failed attempts aren't refunded.
  • Payouts can be denied for rule breaks. Check your account against the rules before you request.
  • 1-Step best-day rule. For orders from 12 October 2026, your best day must be 50% or less of total profit (FTMO objectives page, modified 2 November 2026). Check it before you request.
  • Instant accounts: the intraday trailing max loss doesn't reset when you withdraw. I avoid intraday trailing drawdown for exactly this kind of reason.
  • Third-party EAs and the $400,000 allocation cap can lead to a denied payout (FTMO FAQ, modified 30 October 2026).

FTMO payout FAQ

How long does FTMO take to pay?

FTMO says it reviews a request within 1–2 business days and typically sends the reward within 1–2 business days after the invoice is approved. Mine took about 2–3 days to process each time.

Does FTMO pay every 14 days?

I can't confirm that. FTMO's help centre says you can first request on the 14th or any following day after your first trade, but it states no fixed cycle after the first reward. Some aggregator sites say every 14 days; that's unconfirmed.

Does FTMO pay on demand?

FTMO doesn't use that wording on any help page I read (12 November 2026). After the first reward the pages are silent on frequency, so I won't promise on-demand payouts.

Is the challenge fee refunded?

On the 2-Step, yes: FTMO refunds the initial fee once you request your first reward, to the same payment method. 1-Step and Instant fees aren't refundable, and failed attempts aren't refunded.

What do I need before my first payout?

Day 14 or later after your first trade, approved KYC, all positions and pending orders closed, and at least $20 closed profit ($50 for crypto). Check the rules for your account type too.
