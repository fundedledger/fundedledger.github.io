The five rules that end most accounts

  1. Intraday trailing drawdown: it follows open-profit peaks. Buy end-of-day (futures) or static (CFD). Why
  2. Daily loss limits that count open trades: size so a full stop-out on everything open can't breach the day.
  3. Consistency rules: one big day can raise your target or lock your payout. Keep days even-sized.
  4. Hold-time and news rules: read the funded-account terms, not just the evaluation. Scalping rules
  5. Bot rules: many firms ban shared or commercial bots; Apex reportedly bans all. Bot rules
