Then add the costs that don't show on the sales page: activation fees, reset fees, futures market data fees and platform fees. A cheap challenge with a harsh rule that fails you more often (intraday drawdown, a tight daily limit) can end up costing more than a pricier one with forgiving rules.

How I'd spend a small budget

Buy the cheapest challenge that still has the drawdown you'd choose anyway: end-of-day on futures, static on CFDs. Intraday trailing cost me Apex accounts again and again, and no discount makes up for that. Then get one payout before you buy anything bigger.
