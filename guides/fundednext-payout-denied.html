Denied, delayed or just not there yet?

These are different problems. A delay means the request is in and hasn't landed. A denial means FundedNext refused it. A request you can't make (the transfer option is gone) is usually one of the rules below, not a refusal.

On timing: FundedNext says "99.99% processed within 24 hours", but the same pages show "40hrs" as the average processing time and a chart reading 4 hours, so sources differ. Treat 24 hours as a claim, not a promise (Futures and Flex pages, checked 8 Nov 2026). More in the payout rules guide.

When the firm's own pages disagree

A cap mismatch can look like a short payout. The withdrawal guide (12 Oct 2026) says Rapid is capped at $800 (25K), $1,200 (50K) or $2,500 (100K). The Rapid plan page and the Rapid Daily article show a ladder instead: $600 → $850 (25K), $1,000 → $1,250 (50K), $2,000 → $2,500 (100K) by withdrawal number. I haven't picked a winner. Plan on the lower number and ask support which applies to your account.

A catch that isn't a denial

This is from my own Rapid Pro 25K. FundedNext advertises no buffer rule for Rapid Pro. I requested $800 at a $26,094 balance. The loss floor stayed at $25,100, so my room fell from $994 to $194. The payout arrived, so it wasn't a denial. But after a withdrawal you have less room before the loss limit, and that is worth knowing before you request. The rule is in FundedNext's help center; I felt the wording misled me, and I'm not calling it a scam. Full account: Rapid Pro catch.
