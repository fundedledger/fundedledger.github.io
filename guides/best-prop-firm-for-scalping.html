The Best Prop Firms for Scalping in 2026

Most prop firms say scalping is allowed. The catch is in the fine print: minimum hold times that can wipe out your profits on review, and news rules that make you go flat at the worst moment.

Updated October 2026Written by a funded trader

Short answer: for forex scalping, FTMO is the most often recommended: no meaningful minimum hold time. For futures, Topstep, Apex, Bulenox and FTMO Futures are reported to have no minimum hold time. Tradeify and Lucid have hold-time rules you need to read first.

The two rules that matter to scalpers

  1. Minimum hold timeSome firms require trades, or a share of your profit, to come from trades held longer than a set time. Trades that break it can be removed from your profit when you ask for a payout.
  2. News rulesSome firms make you be flat for a few minutes around major releases, or cap how much profit can come from news trading.
  3. Drawdown typeShort trades still move the floor on intraday trailing drawdown. End-of-day or static drawdown is still safer.
  4. Daily loss limitScalpers take many trades. A tight daily limit can end your day after a bad run.

Before you scalp a funded account

  • Search the rulebook for "minimum", "hold", "seconds", "news" and "high-frequency". These clauses are often buried in the payout terms, not the evaluation rules.
  • Check whether rules change at funding. Several firms allow something in the evaluation and restrict it on the funded account.
  • Mind the drawdown. Scalping NQ on intraday trailing drawdown still moves your floor with every open-profit peak. I lost Apex accounts to exactly that, and I now only buy end-of-day.
