My payout, and the catch to check

On 5 November 2026 I requested $800 on a Rapid Pro 25K and received $720 in USDC about 2 minutes later. That is one payout and I won't stretch it further.

What I'd check before you rely on a Rapid Pro payout: FundedNext advertises no buffer rule for Rapid Pro, and that is documented in its help center. But my balance was $26,094 with the loss floor locked at $25,100, so I had $994 of room. After the $800 request I had $194. The floor didn't move down with the withdrawal. I felt the wording misled me; I'm not calling it a scam, because the payout arrived and the rule is published. Read the full account in the Rapid Pro catch section of my Futures review.

Watch out

  • Rapid Pro room shrinks after a payout. The floor stayed put in my case, so every dollar withdrawn is a dollar less of room (see above).
  • The firm's figures conflict. Caps, the Rapid Pro consistency rule and processing time all differ between pages. Screenshot the page for your plan before you buy.
  • Rapid Daily buffer. You can only withdraw profit above $26,100 / $52,100 / $102,600, and the account ends after the 5th reward.
  • Flex benchmark days. Five benchmark days ($200 a day on 50K/100K, $250 on 150K) come before the first payout, and nothing below $250 can be withdrawn.
  • Direct's 20% perpetual rule. One big day can cap your payouts for the life of the account, on top of the $2,100 buffer and the $800 minimum.
  • Trustpilot payout-hold complaints. Trustpilot has reviews reporting payout holds (28 Oct and 4 Nov 2026) and complaints about terminations for scalping or copy-trading. Those are one-sided user claims I haven't verified, and there are also five-star reviews praising fast payouts.
