Holiday Trading Hours 2026: Futures & Prop Firm Cutoffs

Thanksgiving (Nov 26) and Black Friday (Nov 27) have just passed, but Christmas, New Year and MLK Day are still ahead. A position still open at an early close can get auto-flattened, or count as a rule break. This page lists the holiday dates, the cutoffs Topstep and FTMO have published, and what I couldn't confirm yet.

Prop firm cutoffs

Most futures prop firms make you flat before the session closes. On a holiday the close comes early, so your deadline moves with it. Topstep and FTMO publish their own notices, summarized below. For other firms I haven't confirmed the rule.

FirmHoliday ruleWatch out for
Topstep Close positions 15 minutes before any early close; positions still open at the cutoff are auto-liquidated. Thu Nov 26: close by 11:45 CT. Fri Nov 27: close by 12:00 CT (SIM, Combine, XFA) or 11:25 CT (Live Funded Account). Thu Dec 24: close by 12:00 CT. Topstep's holiday hours article. "Payouts are not available during holiday hours." On the Live Funded Account, Topstep's CME protocol says CME may blend several calendar days into one trading day (for example, Sunday open through Tuesday close). It doesn't apply to Combine or XFA. How to trade the holidays on a funded account

  1. Move your flat-by timeSet an alarm 15 minutes before your firm's early close. Topstep auto-liquidates open positions at its cutoff, which can cost you slippage.
  2. Expect thin marketsFTMO's own notice warns of lower liquidity and wider spreads around the holiday. If you trade at all, trade smaller.
  3. Check how short days countAsk your firm how a shortened session counts toward minimum trading days or consistency rules.
  4. Request payouts before the breakTopstep says payouts are not available during holiday hours. Check your firm's notice before you plan around a payout date.
  5. Or just take the day offA day you don't trade can't breach your account.
