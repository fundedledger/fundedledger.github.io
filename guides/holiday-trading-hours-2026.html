Holiday Trading Hours 2026: Futures & Prop Firm Cutoffs

Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Christmas and New Year all shorten the futures session. On a prop firm account that matters more than usual: a position still open at an early close can get auto-flattened, or count as a rule break. Here's every holiday session through MLK Day, and when the firms want you flat.

Forex and CFDs over the holidays

  • Forex stays open on US holidays, but liquidity is thin. Spreads widen, especially around the daily rollover.
  • Christmas Day and New Year's Day are effectively closed at most brokers. Many close FX and CFDs early on Dec 24 and Dec 31, then reopen a day or two later.
  • Index and commodity CFDs follow the futures hours above, because they're priced off those futures.

How to trade the holidays on a funded account

  1. Move your flat-by timeSet an alarm 15 minutes before the early close. Auto-liquidation can cost you slippage, and some firms count it as a breach.
  2. Expect thin marketsLow volume means wider spreads and sudden spikes. If you trade at all, trade smaller.
  3. Watch the trading-day countA half day can still count as a full trading day, toward your minimum days or your consistency rule.
  4. Request payouts earlyFirms process slower over the holidays. If you qualify, request before the break.
  5. Or just take the day offA day you don't trade can't breach your account.
