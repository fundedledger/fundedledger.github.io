Trailing vs End-of-Day vs Static Drawdown

The drawdown type decides more prop firm accounts than any strategy does. Here's how each one works, the same trade run through all three, and which one I buy after losing accounts to the wrong one.

Updated October 2026Written by a funded trader

Disclosure: some links on this page are affiliate links. If you buy through them I may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Short answer: static is the most forgiving, end-of-day trailing is next, and intraday trailing is the hardest. On futures, buy end-of-day trailing; static is rarely offered. On CFD firms, buy static.

Why I care about this

Intraday trailing drawdown cost me Apex accounts again and again, so I stopped buying it. On futures I now only take end-of-day trailing, and even that has ended funded accounts of mine that were in profit. On CFD firms I only trade static drawdown.

One trade, three drawdown types

Take a 50K account with a $2,000 drawdown, so the floor starts at $48,000. You open a trade that runs to +$1,500 in open profit, then comes back, and you close it at +$200. Balance: $50,200.

Drawdown typeFloor after the tradeRoom leftWhat happened
Intraday trailing$49,500$700The floor followed your open-profit peak ($51,500 − $2,000). A winning trade cost you $1,300 of room.
End-of-day trailing$48,200$2,000The floor only moves at the close, to $50,200 − $2,000. The swing inside the trade didn't matter.
Static$48,000$2,200The floor never moves. You just gained room.

Illustrative example. Exact mechanics vary by firm, and most trailing drawdowns stop trailing once the floor reaches your starting balance.

Why end-of-day still ends profitable accounts

End-of-day is much better than intraday, but it still trails. Say you build an account up to +$3,000 over a week. Your floor has risen with each close, so a few red days can take you back to it, and the account is gone even though you were "in profit" for most of it. That's how I've lost funded accounts on end-of-day drawdown. The fix is the lock: most firms stop trailing once the floor reaches your starting balance, so get there and protect it.
