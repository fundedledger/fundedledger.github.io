NQ or MNQ: how much room do you really have?

The Nasdaq-100 E-mini (NQ) moves $5.00 per 0.25-point tick, or $20 per point. The Micro (MNQ) is one tenth of that: $0.50 per tick, $2 per point. So 10 MNQ equal 1 NQ. These tick values come from third-party broker pages; I could not read CME's contract page, so check them there.

Divide your max loss by $20 and you get how many NQ points your account can lose on one contract. This is plain arithmetic, not a recommendation:

Room = max loss ÷ ($20 × contracts). It ignores fees, slippage and gaps. One NQ tick equals 10 MNQ ticks.

That is why the contract cap matters more than it looks. Running the full 4 NQ on a $2,000 limit leaves 25 points, which a normal NQ session can cover in minutes. Micros let you size in smaller steps: the same $2,000 is 1,000 points on a single MNQ, or 100 points on 10 MNQ (the equivalent of 1 NQ). The firms below allow 30 to 50 micros on a 50K (Apex's micro cap wasn't found). How many contracts you hold is your decision and your risk.

Drawdown fit: end-of-day or intraday?

NQ is fast. On intraday trailing drawdown your floor rises with every open-profit peak, so a spike in your favour followed by a pullback can end the account while you are still in profit. That is what cost me Apex accounts again and again, so on futures I only buy end-of-day trailing. Even that has ended funded accounts of mine that were in profit, but it is still the option I prefer on futures. More in trailing vs end-of-day drawdown.

Watch out: "end-of-day" often means the floor only moves at the close. Tradeify's help center says its drawdown is enforced live against open P&L, while its dashboard figure updates at close, so an NQ spike can fail the account intraday (official). Treat every end-of-day firm the same way until you have read how it enforces the limit. Also note which plan you buy: Lucid Daily and Apex's intraday option use intraday trailing (third-party reports). I stick to LucidFlex and LucidPro.

FAQ

Should I trade NQ or MNQ on a prop account?

That's your call. MNQ is one tenth of NQ ($2 per point against $20), so the same loss limit gives ten times the points of room per contract. The firms above allow 30 to 50 micros on the 50K (Apex's micro cap wasn't found).

Which prop firm is best for Nasdaq futures if I scalp?

Read the hold-time rules first. Tradeify blocks payouts on funded accounts if over 50% of trades and profit come from trades held 10 seconds or less. See best prop firms for scalping.
