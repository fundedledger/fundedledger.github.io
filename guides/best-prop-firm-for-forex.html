Why static drawdown first

A static max loss is a fixed floor under your starting balance. A trailing one moves up as your equity peaks, so your room shrinks when you win. Same percentage, very different account. For the full comparison, read trailing vs end-of-day drawdown. For the other rules side by side, see the prop firm rules cheat sheet.

Not every product at these firms is static. FTMO's 1-Step is end-of-day trailing, Maven's Standard 1-Step is trailing (5% from highest equity), and FXIFY sells trailing programs too. Check the program name before you buy.

FAQ

Is static drawdown always better?

For keeping room as you win, yes: the floor does not rise with your peak. A static 6% floor can still be tighter than a trailing 10% in some setups, so compare the dollars, not the label.
