The one tool detail from my own setup: I trade NQ and gold futures on Tradovate and place the orders from TradingView, so for me Tradovate is the import route, not TradingView.

A journal doesn't enforce your firm's rules. Any rule tracker here works from the data it receives and from rules you enter. Your prop firm's own dashboard is the source of truth for your drawdown, daily loss and payout eligibility.

What to look for

A journal is only useful if the trades get in without manual work. For a prop trader that comes down to three questions:

  1. Does it import my platform?Tradovate, Rithmic, NinjaTrader and MetaTrader are what most firms run on. Check the exact one you trade.
  2. Does it track firm rules?Daily loss limits, drawdown type and profit target, so you see them before the firm's dashboard shows a breach. See trailing vs end-of-day drawdown for why the type matters.
  3. What does it cost per account?Prop traders often run several accounts at once, and some plans cap them.

TraderSync

Who it's for: traders who want AI feedback and mobile apps, and who have confirmed their platform imports cleanly. Its own pricing and broker pages could not be loaded during research, so see their pricing page for plans and limits.

Which should you pick?

FAQ

Do I need a trading journal to pass a prop firm evaluation?

No firm requires one. A journal helps you see which setups and sessions lose money, and rule tracking can warn you before a daily loss limit. Your firm's dashboard still decides whether you've breached.

Which journal is best for futures prop firms?

It depends on your platform. All four list Tradovate in some form (TraderSync's is unconfirmed), and only Tradervue and TradeZella list Rithmic. Check the table above, then confirm on the tool's own site.

Can I journal trades placed from TradingView?

Import from where the trades execute. If your orders route through Tradovate, import from Tradovate. TradeZella and Edgewonk mention TradingView paper trading only.

Will a journal stop me breaking my firm's rules?

No. It can show rule progress, but it doesn't enforce limits and can lag your account. Treat the firm's dashboard as the source of truth.

Are there free options?

Tradervue has been reported to offer a limited free plan, but third-party details may be out of date. For the others I found a trial or money-back guarantee at best, so check each site.
