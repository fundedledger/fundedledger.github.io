What end-of-day drawdown means here

With end-of-day drawdown, your loss floor moves up only when the day closes, based on your closing balance. It does not follow every tick of open profit. That gives a winning trade room to breathe during the session. The full explanation is in trailing vs end-of-day drawdown.

Two things the label does not tell you. First, a firm can set the floor at the end of the day and still check your open loss live, so a floating loss can breach you before the close. Second, the type can change when you move from evaluation to funded. Both show up below.

Where "end-of-day" changes after funding

  • TradeDay Quick Pay End of Day: end-of-day in the evaluation, intraday once funded. The card itself says "End of Day (Intraday Funded Drawdown)". Only Fast Pass stays end-of-day after funding.
  • Earn2Trade: end-of-day in the evaluation and LiveSim, trailing on Live. The $200K and $400K funded plans are fixed instead.
  • Alpha Futures, FundedNext Futures, FTMO Futures: end-of-day in both stages, per their own pages. Each has a catch, below.

If you only buy end-of-day, as I do, the question to ask before you pay is simple: what is the drawdown on the funded account, in the firm's own words?

Firm by firm

Alpha Futures

Verdict: end-of-day on every account, and the help center says so in plain words. It is the cleanest answer for an end-of-day-only buyer on this list. I haven't traded this firm. See the Alpha Futures review. Check Alpha Futures pricing (no code).

Watch out: breach is checked on floating equity. The help article says: "If you break MLL at any point, floating equity or closed balance... will be liquidated." How floating P&L moves the trailing level is not stated. On Zero, the Daily Loss Guard locks you until 6 PM ET the next day. That is a lock, not a breach. Third-party sites show out-of-date prices and drawdowns, so use the firm's pages.

TradeDay

Verdict: buy Fast Pass if you want end-of-day after funding. Quick Pay "End of Day" is end-of-day only in the evaluation. I haven't traded this firm. See the TradeDay review. Check TradeDay pricing (no code confirmed).

A 1-star Trustpilot review (23 July 2026) complains about exactly this switch, and the company replied that it is "clearly outlined". The card does carry the label, but it is easy to skim past. Some third-party pages say TradeDay still sells a static option. Its own cards show only intraday and end-of-day, so I say there is no static option on the current plans.

Earn2Trade

Verdict: an end-of-day evaluation that becomes trailing on Live. Fine if you accept that, a trap if you do not. I haven't traded this firm. See the Earn2Trade review. Check Earn2Trade pricing Use code LEDGER at checkout

How to choose

  • You want end-of-day after funding, with the simplest wording: Alpha Futures, or FundedNext Futures if you accept the live floating-loss breach.
  • You are looking at TradeDay: Fast Pass, not Quick Pay End of Day.
  • You are looking at Earn2Trade: know that Live is trailing before you pay for the evaluation.
  • You are looking at FTMO Futures: read the daily loss limit and withdrawal rules on your plan. Pro has a hard limit.
  • Whatever you pick: compute your room after a withdrawal before you request one.

FAQ

Which futures prop firms have end-of-day drawdown?

By their own pages: Alpha Futures, FundedNext Futures, FTMO Futures, TradeDay Fast Pass and LucidFlex are end-of-day in evaluation and funded stages. Earn2Trade and TradeDay Quick Pay are end-of-day in the evaluation only. See the table.

Does end-of-day drawdown protect me from a spike during the day?

Not always. Alpha Futures, Earn2Trade and FundedNext Flex all say open or floating losses count live. End-of-day sets where the floor moves, not whether a live loss can breach you.

Which plans are end-of-day in the evaluation but not once funded?

TradeDay Quick Pay End of Day (intraday once funded) and Earn2Trade (trailing on Live; the $200K and $400K funded plans are fixed).
