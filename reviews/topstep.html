Topstep Review (2026)

Updated October 2026 · Written by a funded trader

Disclosure: this review contains affiliate links. If you sign up through them I may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Verdict: Topstep is the "steady, by-the-book" choice. The end-of-day loss limit is forgiving, the rules are clear, and the firm has been around since 2012. You pay a monthly subscription until you pass, and the best-day consistency rule can push your real target above the stated one. Best for traders who want structure over speed.

My experience with Topstep

I've bought Topstep Combines, but Topstep hasn't paid me out yet. So this review is based on its published rules and my time in the Combine, not on a payout I've received. Weigh it against the firms that have paid me: FTMO, Lucid Trading and CryptoFundTrader.

  • Max loss limit trails your end-of-day balance and stops trailing once it reaches your starting balance. Touch it and the Combine ends.
  • Consistency rule: your best day can be at most about 55% of your total profit.
  • Pricing: a monthly subscription. Reported prices start around $49/month for the 50K on the standard path, with an activation fee when you pass. Other paths cost more per month but have no activation fee.
  • Profit split: 90/10 in your favor for traders who joined from January 12, 2026.

Third-party sources disagree on some prices and payout caps. Check Topstep's site before you buy.

How the consistency rule changes your target

On a 50K Combine, the target is $3,000. If your best day is $2,000, the 55% rule means you need $2,000 ÷ 0.55 ≈ $3,640 in total. A big day early on effectively raises your target. That's why many traders cap their daily profit while in the Combine.

Getting paid: the Express Funded Account

After you pass, you trade an Express Funded Account. Two payout paths are reported: a Standard path (5 winning days of $150 or more) and a Consistency path (3 trading days with your best day kept within a 40% share). Payout caps per request vary by account and path.

Pros

  • End-of-day trailing loss limit: no intraday trailing
  • Long track record (since 2012)
  • No deadline to pass
  • Clear, well-documented rules

Cons

  • Monthly subscription: slow passes get expensive
  • Best-day consistency rule during the Combine
  • Activation fee on the cheapest path
  • Pricing differs by path, which is confusing

Who Topstep is for

A good fit: newer futures traders who want forgiving drawdown and no deadline, and who trade steady daily P&L.

Not a good fit: traders who want a one-time fee, or who make most of their profit on a few big days.
