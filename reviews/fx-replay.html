I've used FX Replay for backtesting and had a good experience. That's all I'm claiming from first-hand use: I'm not stating which markets, plan or results, and nothing below is a backtest result. Everything else on this page, including plans, prices, limits and complaints, comes from FX Replay's public pages and help center and from Trustpilot, checked on 2 November 2026.

Markets and data

  • Coverage: more than 300 assets on all plans: forex, indices (as CFDs, such as US30 and US NAS 100), metals including XAUUSD, energy, bonds, agriculturals, some crypto and a few major stocks. Intermediate adds more US stocks.
  • Futures: Pro adds all futures (NQ, ES, YM and the micros, plus CME contracts such as GC), history from 2012.
  • History: most forex, indices and metals go back to 2003. Session-data retention depends on plan: 1 week, 6 months or unlimited.
  • Data type: OHLCV candles from 1 minute to monthly; Pro adds seconds candles (1s and 5s) on supported assets. Futures volume is real exchange volume. The homepage talks about "tick-precise" replay, but the help center describes candles, so don't buy it expecting tick data.
  • Platform: runs in the browser. I found no desktop or mobile app.

Using it for prop firm practice

FX Replay has a "Prop Firm Session" mode where you set a profit target, a maximum daily loss and a maximum total loss, and a rules tab shows your status. Skipping ahead is disabled in these sessions. The homepage also mentions configurable spreads and commissions. The order window supports stop loss, take profit (with partial take profit) and auto break-even.

Where it stops: the help article documents only those three rules. I found no firm presets, and no documentation of trailing or end-of-day drawdown, so it won't replicate a firm like Lucid Trading or Topstep on its own. Track the drawdown floor yourself as in the drawdown guide, and treat your firm's dashboard as the source of truth. Replay also ignores real slippage and nerves.

How it compares: see the backtesting software guide for FX Replay against Forex Tester Online and TradingView Bar Replay. In short, FX Replay is the only one of the three with a built-in prop firm session; Forex Tester claims deeper tick data, and TradingView Bar Replay is included if you already pay for it.

Is there a discount?

FX Replay's affiliate page says referred users get 15% off. I couldn't confirm how it's applied, which plans or billing periods it covers or how long it lasts, so check your price at checkout and don't count on it.

Watch-outs

  • No public refund or cancellation terms. Its terms and refund page URLs returned errors when I checked, and I found nothing public on auto-renewal. You can't read the terms before you pay. Ask support and screenshot the answer.
  • Billing complaints. Trustpilot shows 4.1 from 657 reviews (2 November 2026; it was 4.2 from 652 on 24 October). Recent 1-star reviews mention an auto-renewal with an unanswered refund request and difficulty unsubscribing. I read only the first page of reviews, and Trustpilot isn't fact-checked.
  • Lag. Some reviews from September and October 2026 report lag, freezes and candle-loading problems. That may relate to the chart migration below.
  • Chart migration. FX Replay is retiring its old charts for a "New Chart", and existing drawings and layouts don't carry over.
  • Slow replies to negatives. It replied to 29% of negative Trustpilot reviews, typically after more than a month. Many positive reviews praise named support staff.
  • Candles, not ticks, and futures cost extra. If you need NQ or ES, you need Pro.

Who FX Replay is for

A good fit: forex and gold traders who want to practise a strategy on candles, rehearse a profit target and loss limits, and start on a free plan before paying.

Not a good fit: traders who need tick-level futures history to test intraday trailing drawdown (compare Forex Tester Online), or anyone who wants guaranteed refund terms in writing before paying.

FAQ

Is FX Replay free?

There's a free Beginner plan with no card required that doesn't expire, with limits on sessions, trades, indicators and charts. Intermediate and Pro are paid.

Does FX Replay have NQ and ES futures?

Yes, but only on Pro. Futures data starts in 2012, as candles, with seconds candles available on supported assets.

Does the Prop Firm Simulator copy my firm's rules?

Not on its own. It documents a profit target, a daily loss limit and a total loss limit. I found no documentation of trailing or end-of-day drawdown, so track your firm's exact rules yourself.

FX Replay's affiliate page says referred users get 15% off. Check your price at checkout, because I couldn't confirm how it's applied.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

I couldn't find public refund, cancellation or auto-renewal terms. The pricing page says you can upgrade, downgrade or stay free at any time. Ask support before paying and keep a copy of the answer.
