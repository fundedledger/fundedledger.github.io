How the drawdown works

Every current plan uses end-of-day trailing drawdown, not intraday trailing. The max loss level is recalculated once a day from your end-of-day balance, and it stops trailing at your starting balance plus $100.

Read this part: your live equity is checked against that level all day. FundedNext's help center says a floating loss that breaches the max loss limit during a live trade breaches your account instantly, even if you would have closed the trade up. The level is end-of-day; the check is live. On a Flex 50K, $1,500 is 75 NQ points on one contract. More in trailing vs end-of-day drawdown.

Payouts

  • Flex: first payout after 5 benchmark days (profit days of $200 on 50K/100K, $250 on 150K) and $500 cycle profit. Minimum $250, maximum 50% of profit up to $1,500 / $2,500 / $4,000. 95% split. No consistency rule on the funded account.
  • Rapid Pro: every 3 days, with 40% consistency and $500 cycle profit. Minimum $250, maximum $800 / $1,200 / $2,500. 90%.
  • Rapid Daily: daily, once your end-of-day balance is above a buffer ($26,100 / $52,100 / $102,600) and you have $500 cycle profit. Only profit above the buffer is withdrawable. Minimum $250, same caps as Rapid Pro. 90%.
  • Direct: $52,100 buffer, $800 cycle profit and the 20% perpetual consistency rule. $800 to $1,200 per payout. 90%. KYC before the first withdrawal.
  • Account lifetime: Flex, Rapid Daily and Direct accounts conclude after 5 Performance Rewards. I couldn't confirm the Rapid Pro lifetime. On Flex 50K that's at most 5 x $1,500 = $7,500 gross, before the 95% split.
  • Speed: FundedNext says payouts are processed within 24 hours. See the watch-outs below.

Source: Futures help center, checked 4 November 2026. The first withdrawal on Flex locks the max loss level at start balance + $100.

Trading rules

  • No overnight or weekend holds. Close everything by 3:10 PM CT. Positions are auto-closed; trading resumes at 5:00 PM CT.
  • Inactivity: an account is breached after 30 calendar days with no trade (on Direct, at least one trade per week, Monday to Friday).
  • Micro-scalping: trades closed within 10 seconds trigger a warning at 30% of profit; at 40% or more that profit is deducted for the cycle (the account isn't terminated).
  • Prohibited: platform-error exploits, account sharing, spoofing, layering, grid, wash trading, latency arbitrage, reverse hedging, hedging correlated instruments, trading within 2% of CME price limits, and unauthorised copy trading.
  • Platforms: NinjaTrader and Tradovate; Direct also lists TradingView.
  • Contracts (Flex): 3 minis or 30 micros on 50K, 5 / 50 on 100K, 8 / 80 on 150K.
  • Who can buy: the US isn't on the restricted-country list. A long list of other countries is, so check the help center's restricted countries article.
