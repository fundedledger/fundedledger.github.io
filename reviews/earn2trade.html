My experience with Earn2Trade

I haven't traded Earn2Trade, and it hasn't paid me. Here's how I'd judge it after 100+ evals elsewhere (40+ passed). Everything below comes from Earn2Trade's website and help center, checked on 4 November 2026, plus public reviews where I say so.

What I can tell you is my own rule for futures. Intraday trailing drawdown cost me Apex accounts again and again, so I stopped buying it, and on futures I only buy end-of-day trailing. Even that has ended funded accounts of mine that were in profit. Earn2Trade's evaluation is end-of-day, but its Live accounts are trailing, so my rule only covers part of the journey here.

What Earn2Trade is

It calls itself "an educational company headquartered in Sheridan, Wyoming" that connects successful candidates with proprietary trading firms for funding, and says it needs no financial licence. It says it has been in business since 2016 and funded 6,400+ traders in 2025 (its own claims). The pages I read don't name the funding firm. Futures only.

Drawdown: the nuance that matters

Earn2Trade calls its evaluation and LiveSim drawdown end-of-day, and that part is accurate: the minimum balance rises $1 for every $1 of end-of-day profit, is adjusted after the close, and stops at the starting balance. But the help center also says that "if an open position dips your account balance below the minimum account balance, your evaluation attempt will fail". So a deep floating loss in the middle of the day can fail you even if you would have recovered by the close.

  • Evaluation and LiveSim: end-of-day drawdown, with floating losses counted in real time.
  • Live: trailing drawdown, which "increases dollar-for-dollar with your positive account performance during the trading day until it reaches the starting balance". Only the $200K and $400K funded TCP accounts use fixed drawdown. Older third-party sources call everything trailing; I trust the help center article dated 10 August 2026.
  • Daily loss limit: counts open, closed and commission P&L from 5pm to 5pm CT. Reaching it "even if you don't close your position" fails the evaluation and suspends trading. It is not a pause until tomorrow.

Worked example 1 (illustrative numbers, TCP25). You start at $25,000 with a $1,500 drawdown, so the minimum balance is $23,500. You close day one at +$400, which lifts the minimum to $23,900. On day two a trade runs against you by $1,550, taking the balance to $23,850. It would have recovered by the close, but the minimum balance was touched in real time, so the attempt fails.

Worked example 2 (illustrative numbers, TCP25). The same trade dips only $560 from the day's start. You are nowhere near the $1,500 drawdown, but the daily loss limit is $550, measured including open P&L. At $560 down, the evaluation fails and trading is suspended.

Against my own rule (end-of-day trailing only on futures), the evaluation fits and Live does not. See trailing vs end-of-day drawdown.

The other rules that matter

These are the rules I'd read before paying.

Consistency (evaluation only)

No single trading day can account for 30% or more of your total profit. Going over doesn't fail you; you just need to trade more until the day is under 30%. There is no consistency rule on LiveSim or Live, and no minimum trading days.

Worked example (illustrative numbers, TCP25): your total profit is $1,750 and one day made $600. That day is about 34% of the total, so it's too big. You'd need total profit above $2,000 before that $600 day falls below 30%.

Hours and overnight

The help center says "you CANNOT hold overnight positions". All positions and working orders must be closed by 3:50pm CT until the 5pm CT reopening. Whether the same applies on Live accounts isn't stated, and neither is a funded-account weekend or inactivity rule.

News

Allowed. The help center says there are "no restrictions on trading around economic announcements" (13 November 2025).

Bots and copy trading

Copy trading and batch execution across multiple accounts are not permitted on any program, including after you pass, and Earn2Trade may deny accounts or withdrawals, with no refund. There is no dedicated EA article, but the prohibited-conduct page bans software, artificial intelligence or ultra-fast data entry that could "manipulate the trading environment or provide an unfair advantage", and an unrealistic number of contracts in a day. A 4 November Trustpilot 1-star says copy trading was alleged only at payout (unverified; the rule is in the help center).

Payouts

Payouts are processed once a week, on Wednesdays. You email the request by 2:00pm CT the previous Friday; late requests may wait another week. The minimum is $100 net. Before each request you need five profitable days (not consecutive), each above a minimum daily profit: TCP25 $100, TCP50 $150, TCP100 $200, GM50 $150, GM100 $200, GM150 $250, GM200 $350.

No per-request maximum is stated. LiveSim has a cumulative limit before you're moved to Live: $1,750 on TCP25, $3,000 on TCP50, $6,000 on TCP100 and $5,000 on every GM size. It is "not a limit on any single withdrawal".
