My experience with Alpha Futures

I haven't traded Alpha Futures, and it hasn't paid me. Everything on this page comes from the firm's own website and help center and from public reviews, all checked in October 2026. I have no pass, no payout certificate and no experience of its support to show you.

What I can tell you is what I look for in a futures firm. After intraday trailing drawdown cost me Apex accounts again and again, I only buy end-of-day trailing on futures. Alpha Futures says none of its accounts use intraday trailing, which is why I looked at it. Even end-of-day trailing has ended funded accounts of mine that were in profit, so it's still not forgiving.

For comparison, Lucid Trading has paid me, and I can say so. Alpha Futures has not.

Watch out: July 2026 and payout complaints

Watch out: on 12 July 2026 Alpha Futures ended its NinjaTrader partnership. New NinjaTrader/Tradovate accounts are no longer available, existing Zero, Advanced and Direct accounts were moved to its own AlphaTrader platform, and the Premium plan was discontinued. Reports differ on whether Premium customers got only their purchase price back or their pending payouts too. Separately, Trustpilot reviews from October 2026 allege payouts pending for months and accounts closed with refunds not received. Trustpilot doesn't verify these claims, but they point the same way.
  • Payout speed is contested. The homepage claims same-day payouts once you hit the milestone. The FAQ says approved payouts arrive "within five business days or less". Some Trustpilot reviewers report waits of 10 days up to several months. Plan on the slower figure.
  • One complaint is partly explained by the rules. A reviewer called the payout buffer "hidden". Each request is capped at 50% of account profit and you keep 90% of it. Both are official rules, set out below.
  • Rules change often. The activation fee was dropped, plans were restructured and Standard got a new split and scaling in 2026. This page can go stale; check the current rules before you pay.
  • No licence information found. I found no regulator warnings, but I didn't search every regulator database, and I found no evidence of registration either.

What it costs

Evaluations are monthly subscriptions that rebill until you pass or cancel in your dashboard. After you pass, there is no subscription as a Qualified trader. A failed evaluation restores the starting balance at the next rebill, or you can buy a paid reset without changing your rebill date. Payouts

  • Split: 90% to you.
  • Frequency: up to 4 times a month, each time after 5 winning days of $200 or more (they don't need to be consecutive).
  • Size: each request is up to 50% of account profit, capped by your plan. Advanced allows up to $15,000 per request.
  • Minimums: Zero $200, Standard $500, Direct $500, Advanced $1,000.
  • Direct: profit targets for the first and later payouts are listed on the help center, and leftover profit does not carry over. Its 50K maximum withdrawal differs between two help articles (sources differ), so check the current figure.
  • Speed: see the "Watch out" section. Treat "within five business days" as the realistic promise, and read recent reviews before you rely on it.

Who Alpha Futures is for

A possible fit: discretionary futures traders who want end-of-day drawdown, can pass within a month or two, and accept the payout-delay risk. Start with a small account and request a first payout early, so you learn how fast it really pays.

Not a good fit: bot or hedging traders, anyone who needs NinjaTrader, and anyone who needs a firm with a proven payout record of their own. If that's you, Lucid Trading (which has paid me) is the safer comparison.

FAQ

Does Alpha Futures actually pay out?

I don't know first-hand: I haven't traded it and it hasn't paid me. The firm publishes payout rules and says it paid out $25M+ in two months (its own statement, reported second-hand). But Trustpilot reviewers also report long delays. Sources differ, so test with a small first payout.

How fast are Alpha Futures payouts?

The homepage claims same-day payouts, while the FAQ says approved payouts arrive within five business days or less. Some reviewers report waits of 10 days up to several months. Use the slower figure when you plan.

Is Alpha Futures' drawdown trailing?

Yes, but end-of-day trailing, not intraday. The firm states none of its accounts use intraday trailing. It follows your highest end-of-day balance and stops trailing at your starting balance.

Can I use NinjaTrader or Tradovate with Alpha Futures?

Not for new accounts. Alpha Futures ended its NinjaTrader partnership on 12 July 2026. You can use AlphaTrader, TradingView (via Plus500 connectivity), WealthCharts, Quantower or DeepChart.

Is there a promo code or an activation fee?

I don't have a code to give you, so I'm not listing one. There is no activation fee on accounts bought after 8 July 2026. Older Advanced Qualified accounts were charged $149.
