The rules that matter

These are the rules most likely to end an account. Every worked example below is our own arithmetic, an example and not a real trade.

1. The end-of-day trailing drawdown

This is the main reason to look at FTMO Futures. Drawdown is measured on equity (balance plus open P&L minus commissions). The floor is set once per trading day from your highest prior closing balance minus the drawdown amount. It only moves up, and once it reaches your starting balance it locks there permanently. Open profit during the day doesn't raise it; only your close does. The breach itself is measured on live equity at any point, so a deep intraday dip below the floor still counts.

Example (Pro 50K, $3,000 drawdown):

  • Day 1: floor is $47,000. You close at $51,000.
  • Day 2: floor moves up to $48,000. You close at $53,000.
  • Day 3: $53,000 minus $3,000 would be $50,000, which equals your starting balance, so the floor locks at $50,000 for good.
  • From then on your cushion is simply your balance minus $50,000. At a $53,000 close that's $3,000; at $56,000 it's $6,000.

Growth 50K has a $2,000 drawdown, so it locks after only $2,000 of closed profit. This is the same end-of-day type that has ended funded accounts of mine while in profit, so don't mistake it for safe. More in trailing vs end-of-day drawdown.

2. The consistency rule (Evaluation only)

Your best day can't be more than 40% (Growth) or 50% (Pro) of your total closed profit. Going over is not a breach: you just keep trading until the best day drops under the limit. FTMO states there is no consistency rule or profit target once you're Sim-Funded. The catch is that a big early day forces more trading days.

Example (Growth 50K, $3,000 target, 40%): day 1 you make $2,000 and day 2 $1,000. Total $3,000, so the target is hit, but your best day is 66.7% of profit. You haven't passed. If day 1 stays your best day, you need $2,000 ÷ 0.40 = $5,000 total profit. On Pro 50K a $2,500 best day also needs $5,000 total. FTMO's minimum possible pass is 3 trading days on Growth and 2 on Pro.

3. Pro's hard daily loss limit, and the full-withdrawal trap

Pro has a hard daily loss limit in the Evaluation ($1,000, $1,500 or $2,000 by size), measured on equity against the balance at the start of the trading day. Breaching it fails the Evaluation, and on a Sim-Funded Pro account it's a hard limit too. Growth has none in the Evaluation and a soft one when funded.

Pro also lets you request up to 100% of your profit above the starting balance. FTMO's own FAQ warns that a full withdrawal can breach the maximum drawdown. Example (Pro 50K, drawdown already locked at $50,000): you're at $53,000 and request the $3,000 of profit. Your balance drops to $50,000, which is exactly the floor. Your cushion is $0, and the next small loss breaches the account. If you want to keep trading, request less than the maximum.

4. Flat by 4:10pm ET

No overnight or weekend positions. Everything must be closed and every resting order (stops, limits, brackets) cancelled before 4:10pm ET, or at market close if that's sooner. The platform auto-liquidates, but FTMO says not to rely on it. A trading day runs from 6:00pm ET to 4:10pm ET the next calendar day. If you swing trade, or you like to leave a bracket working into the evening, this is the rule that ends your day.

Also allowed or banned: news trading is allowed. Bots are allowed if they are yours and not high-frequency; a shared off-the-shelf EA risks denial of your Sim-Funded account. Copying your own trades across your own FTMO accounts is allowed, copying others is not, and hedging between accounts is banned. FTMO's forbidden practices are broad and judgment-based, with penalties up to forfeited rewards. Five US states are excluded (Arkansas, Delaware, Louisiana, Montana, South Carolina), plus many countries.

Payouts

You request from a Sim-Funded account once you have enough qualifying days in the payout cycle. A qualifying day is a day with at least a minimum closed profit, and the threshold depends on plan and size:

  • How much: Growth lets you request up to 50% of profit above the starting balance; Pro up to 100%. Both are capped per request (see the plans table). FTMO's example: $4,000 of profit on Growth means you may request $2,000.
  • Split: 90/10 per FTMO's rules page, applied after caps. $2,000 requested pays $1,800. FTMO's launch blog once said Pro keeps 100%; the rules page is the more detailed source and says 90/10, so that's what I've used.
  • Cadence: "paid every 4 days" on FTMO's landing page means 4 qualifying days, not a calendar schedule.
  • Minimum and methods: $20 minimum, paid in USD by Wise, Revolut bank wire, Visa Direct or Mastercard Send (not for US clients). Requesting closes your open positions and pending orders.
  • Speed: FTMO doesn't state a processing time for Futures; requests are "pending FTMO's approval". My CFD payouts took 2–3 days, but I won't apply that to a different product.

FAQ

Is FTMO Futures a one-time fee or a subscription?

A monthly subscription, charged at purchase and on the same date each month. There is no activation fee, and passing the Evaluation cancels the subscription. Sim-Funded accounts are not charged. Checked 5 November 2026.

What is the difference between Growth and Pro?

Growth is cheaper, has no daily loss limit in the Evaluation, uses a 40% consistency rule and lets you request up to 50% of profit per payout (caps $2,500 to $4,000). Pro has a hard daily loss limit, a 50% consistency rule, lets you request up to 100% of profit and has caps of $5,000 to $8,000. Both pay a 90/10 split per FTMO's rules page.

Can I hold FTMO Futures positions overnight?

No. Everything must be closed, and all resting orders (stops, limits, brackets) cancelled, before 4:10pm ET each day. FTMO says not to rely on the platform's auto-liquidation.

Can I use a bot or trade copier?

Your own legitimate, non-high-frequency bot is allowed; a shared off-the-shelf EA risks denial of your Sim-Funded account. Copying your own trades across your own FTMO Futures accounts is allowed; copying someone else's is not.

How fast does FTMO Futures pay?

FTMO doesn't state a processing time for Futures; payout requests are pending FTMO's approval. I haven't traded Futures, and no Futures payout-speed data exists yet. Payout methods are Wise, Revolut bank wire, Visa Direct and Mastercard Send (not for US clients), with a $20 minimum.

Is there an FTMO Futures discount code?

No. FTMO says its affiliate code does not change the price, and we hold no discount code for Futures. Our FTMO discount code page tracks FTMO's current offers.
