The drawdown: end-of-day trailing

This is the main reason to consider FTMO Futures. Per FTMO's rules, the drawdown is measured on equity (balance plus open P&L minus commissions) and works like this:

  • The floor is set once per trading day, from your highest prior closing balance minus the drawdown amount.
  • It only moves up, and it locks at your starting balance. After that you have a fixed cushion.
  • No intraday trailing. Open profit during the day doesn't raise the floor. Only your close does.

My own rule on futures is simple: I only buy end-of-day trailing. Intraday trailing cost me Apex accounts again and again, so I stopped buying it. End-of-day trailing has still ended funded accounts of mine that were in profit, so it isn't painless, but it's the best option futures firms offer. By FTMO's description, this fits that rule. Again, that's from FTMO's rules, not from trading it. The exact drawdown amount per plan and size is unconfirmed (check FTMO). More in trailing vs end-of-day drawdown.

Watch out

  • It's Beta. FTMO says parameters, pricing and availability are subject to change. The product launched in September 2026.
  • Monthly billing keeps running until you pass or cancel. Resets cost extra.
  • Pro has a hard daily loss limit. Breaching it fails the Evaluation and, once funded, may terminate the account (per FTMO's rules summary, unconfirmed).
  • Evaluation consistency rule. Your best day must stay under 40% or 50% of total profit, which can force extra trading days.
  • Flat by 4:10pm ET, resting stops and brackets included. FTMO says not to rely on auto-liquidation.
  • No copy trading. I have used a trade copier elsewhere, but I can't tell you it works here, and FTMO lists copy trading as forbidden.
  • Payout gaps. First-payout timing is not stated, and there is no Futures payout proof from me or anyone I can cite.
  • Simulated funds. All accounts are demo accounts. Payouts come from FTMO, not a live market account.
  • Trustpilot isn't futures-specific. FTMO overall is 4.8 from about 55,000 reviews (checked 31 October 2026), but that is mostly the CFD business, and Trustpilot's summary mentions slow support after technical errors.
  • US restrictions. Five US states are excluded, and US clients need a W-9 to be paid.

FAQ

Does FTMO Futures pay out?

I don't know yet. I haven't traded it, and I have no Futures payout proof. FTMO's CFD 2-Step has paid me $26,138.16 across four 2026 payouts, but that is a different product.

What is FTMO Futures' drawdown?

End-of-day trailing on equity. The floor is set from your highest prior closing balance, only moves up, and locks at your starting balance. There is no intraday trailing. Exact amounts per plan and size are unconfirmed, so check FTMO.

What's the difference between Growth and Pro?

Growth has no daily loss limit in the Evaluation, 4 qualifying days per payout and payouts of up to 50% of available profit, with caps of $2,500–$4,000. Pro has a hard daily loss limit, 5 qualifying days, up to 100% of available profit per cycle and caps of $5,000–$8,000. As of 31 October 2026.

Is there an FTMO Futures discount code?

No. FTMO says its affiliate code does not change the price, and I don't hold a discount code for Futures.

Can I use TradingView or a trade copier?

TradingView is a supported platform (alongside NinjaTrader and Tradovate). Copy trading is prohibited, so don't assume a trade copier is allowed. Check FTMO's Forbidden Trading Practices first.
