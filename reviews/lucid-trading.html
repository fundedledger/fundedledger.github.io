Trading the end-of-day drawdown

Coming from CFD firms, where I only trade static drawdown, Lucid's end-of-day trailing drawdown took me some time to get used to. It's smaller than a CFD firm's 10% (on a 50K account it's $2,000), and it moves up with your closing balance, so giving back gains can still end an account.

Once you understand it, you build your risk management to fit it. The principles:

  • Size against the room you have left, not the account size. On a 50K account with $2,000 of drawdown, your real account is $2,000.
  • Intraday swings don't move the floor. Only your closing balance does, so you can let a trade breathe during the day. Just don't close the day far below your high.
  • Protect the floor until it stops trailing. Once it locks at your starting balance, you have a fixed cushion.

Lucid never uses intraday trailing, the kind that kept costing me Apex accounts. (How the drawdown types compare.)
