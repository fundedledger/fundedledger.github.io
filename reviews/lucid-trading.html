The drawdown: the one thing to get right

Coming from CFD firms, where I only trade static drawdown, Lucid's end-of-day trailing drawdown took me some time to get used to. It's smaller than a CFD firm's 10%, and it moves up with your closing balance, so giving back gains can still end an account.

Once you understand it, you build your risk management to fit it: size positions against the remaining room, not the account size, and protect the floor until it stops trailing. Lucid never uses intraday trailing, the kind that kept costing me Apex accounts. (How the drawdown types compare.)

Who Lucid Trading is for

A good fit: futures day traders, especially NQ and gold, who want to get funded fast and paid fast, and who can trade inside a trailing end-of-day limit.

Not a good fit: traders who need a big, static drawdown. A CFD firm's 2-Step like FTMO suits that better.
