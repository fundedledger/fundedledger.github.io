Pick the 2-Step, not the 1-Step

The 2-Step's 10% max loss is static: the floor never moves. The 1-Step and Instant programs use a 6% trailing limit, smaller and harder to keep. On CFD firms I only trade static drawdown, which is why I've only bought the 2-Step here. (Why the drawdown type matters.)

The red flags, and how to weigh them

I hadn't seen these until I researched this review. You should know about them before you buy:

  • Swiss regulator warning. FINMA put cryptofundtrader.com on its public warning list in August 2024, with the reason "not entered in commercial register." The firm says it operates through a Swiss company registered in Zug under a different name. FINMA says a listing doesn't automatically mean an activity is unlawful, but the warning is still up.
  • No Trustpilot score. Trustpilot removed the rating for breaching its guidelines and deleted fake reviews.
  • No independent payout record. The firm says it has paid over $20 million, but no third party has verified it. My own ~$15,000 is one real data point, not an audit.
  • Hidden add-on costs. Two paid add-ons, each 20% of the challenge price, aren't on the pricing page and can raise the real cost by up to 40%.
  • No refunds or reset discounts on evaluation fees.

Put together: I've been paid reliably, but you're trusting the firm more than you would FTMO. Protect yourself by keeping account sizes modest and withdrawing as soon as you qualify.
