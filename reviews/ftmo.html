Which one to pick: the 2-Step. The 1-Step is cheaper and pays 90% from the start, but its max loss trails, its daily cap is a tight 3%, and its fee isn't refunded. The 2-Step's static floor means a good run builds you real room above your limit, and it doesn't drag the floor up behind you.

The daily loss limit catches people out

FTMO's daily limit is measured from the start of the day: your previous day's closing balance minus the daily loss amount, reset at midnight Prague time. It counts closed trades, open positions, commissions and swaps.

In practice: on a 100K account the daily limit is $5,000. If you're down $3,000 on closed trades and holding a position that's $2,000 underwater, you've hit it. The account fails even if that position would have recovered. Size positions so a full stop-out on everything open can't take you past the day's limit.

How I'd approach the challenge

  1. Pick the 2-StepA static 10% floor and a refundable fee beat the 1-Step's cheaper price.
  2. Respect the daily limitCount open risk, not just closed losses, against the 5%.
  3. Don't rushThere's no hard deadline on the 2-Step. Passing both phases took me roughly two weeks.
  4. Phase 2 is half the work5% with the same limits. Keep the same sizing that got you through phase 1.

Getting paid

Once funded, you can request your first reward 14 days after your first funded trade. In my experience each payout then took about 2–3 days to process. My four 2026 payouts ranged from $4,604 to $9,265, and none had problems.

The split starts at 80% on the 2-Step and can rise to 90% through FTMO's scaling plan, which also grows your account size over time if you stay profitable.

FAQ

Does FTMO actually pay out?

Yes. It has paid me four times in 2026, $26,138.16 in total, with each payout taking about 2–3 days. The certificates are above.

Should I buy the 1-Step or the 2-Step?

The 2-Step for most traders: its 10% max loss is static, the daily limit is 5% instead of 3%, and the fee is refunded with your first reward. The 1-Step only makes sense if you need the 90% split from day one and can trade inside a trailing limit.

How long does it take to pass?

It took me roughly two weeks. The 2-Step has a minimum number of trading days per phase and, in most reports, no hard deadline.

Is the challenge fee refunded?

On the 2-Step, yes, with your first reward. On the 1-Step, no.

Can I trade futures at FTMO?

FTMO launched FTMO Futures in 2026 as a beta, with end-of-day drawdown. It's separate from the CFD challenge and has no payout history yet. See new prop firms in 2026.
