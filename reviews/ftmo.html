FTMO Review (2026)

Updated October 2026 · Written by a funded trader

Disclosure: this review contains affiliate links. If you sign up through them I may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Verdict: FTMO is the best-known forex and CFD prop firm. The 2-Step challenge has the friendliest drawdown of any firm I cover: a fixed 10% floor that never trails. The trade-off is two phases to pass and a strict daily loss limit that counts open trades. If you trade futures, look at Apex or Topstep instead.

My experience with FTMO

I've traded FTMO's 2-Step challenge on both 50K and 100K accounts. Passing took me roughly two weeks, and once I was funded, payouts took about 2–3 days to process. FTMO is one of three firms that have actually paid me, along with Lucid Trading and CryptoFundTrader.

On CFD firms I only trade static drawdown, and the 2-Step's fixed 10% floor is exactly that. After losing futures accounts to trailing drawdown, a floor that never moves makes a real difference.

The daily loss limit catches people out

FTMO's daily limit is measured from the start of the day: your previous day's closing balance minus the daily loss amount, reset at midnight Prague time. It counts closed trades, open positions, commissions and swaps. A trade you haven't closed that's down far enough breaks it, even if it would have recovered.

Why the 2-Step's fixed drawdown matters

On a 100K 2-Step account you fail only if equity drops below $90,000, even if you've been up to $108,000 in the meantime. Compare that with trailing drawdown, where the $108,000 peak would have moved your floor up with it. (Drawdown types explained.)

Pros

  • Fixed 10% max loss on the 2-Step
  • Well-known firm with a long payout record
  • A choice of 1-Step or 2-Step
  • One-time fee, not a subscription

Cons

  • Forex/CFD only: not for futures traders
  • Daily loss limit counts open trades
  • The 2-Step takes two phases to pass
  • The 1-Step has trailing drawdown and a 3% daily cap

Who FTMO is for

A good fit: forex, index and gold traders who want the most forgiving overall drawdown and don't mind a two-phase evaluation.

Not a good fit: futures traders, or anyone who holds big open losses during the day.
