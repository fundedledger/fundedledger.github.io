What E8 is

E8 Markets (E8 Funding) says it was founded in 2021, is based in Dallas, Texas, and has 300,000+ registrations. It claims $71 million processed in payouts; that is its own number and unaudited.

E8 calls itself a simulated trading platform: even funded accounts are demo accounts. It says it is not a broker and has no licence, and there is no path to live capital.

It sells four products. Zero and Signature are CME futures; One and Pro are CFD and crypto-style "perpetual futures". This review covers the futures side. (Pro has static drawdown, One has dynamic.)

The rules that matter

Most rules are ordinary. Daily loss: none on Zero; Signature has a soft 2% "Daily Pause" in the funded stage that stops trading until 00:00 server time without breaching. News trading is allowed. Zero has a 40% best-day rule in the challenge; Signature's challenge has none.

Three rules are where I'd expect accounts to end. The examples use my own illustrative numbers, not real trades.

1. Flat by 15:10 CT, and the drawdown line

Overnight and weekend holding is not allowed on Zero or Signature futures. E8's futures help center says positions are force-closed at 15:10 CT every day, and trading hours run from 17:15 to 15:10 CT.

The drawdown is end-of-day dynamic. The loss line equals your highest end-of-day balance minus the drawdown amount, and it only moves once a day at the close. But E8 says that if equity or balance "reaches/falls below the loss level", the account is permanently closed. So the line is updated end-of-day, but the test is live.

Example (illustrative, Signature 50K): you start at $50,000 with a $2,000 drawdown, so the loss line is $48,000. You close day 1 at +$1,000 (balance $51,000). The line moves to $49,000 at the close. On day 2 a trade floats down and equity touches $48,900 at 11:00 CT. That's below the line, so the account is closed even if you would have recovered by the close. Closing back above the line afterward doesn't undo the breach. The line locks at the $50,000 starting balance once closed profit equals the drawdown, or at your first payout.

2. Payout caps, buffers and the 35% rule

Signature requires a permanent buffer equal to the drawdown. For example, $2,000 on a 50K, which can never be requested. Only profit from the current cycle counts, and to request a payout your best day can't exceed 35% of the profit (the best-day check restarts after each payout). Breaching the 35% rule doesn't lose the account; you just keep trading.

Example (illustrative, Signature 50K bought after 1 October 2026): balance $54,000, so $4,000 profit. Best day was $1,200, which is 30%: passes. Minus the $2,000 buffer, $2,000 looks requestable, but the cap for payouts 1 and 2 on a 50K is $1,250 per request. Whether the cap applies before or after the split isn't stated, so check. And if your best day had been $1,600 (40%), you'd keep trading until the ratio dropped.

Zero works differently. After your first payout the loss line is fixed at the initial balance, so your buffer is the gap between your balance and the starting balance. E8's own example: a Zero 100K at $105,000 requests $4,000, leaves $101,000, and has a $1,000 buffer. That example only works with a cap above $4,000. On Zero Starter the 100K cap is $1,500; on Zero Max it's $5,000. Caps are by account size, not payout number. Zero Starter: $600 / $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,000 (25K / 50K / 100K / 150K). Zero Max: $1,500 / $3,000 / $5,000 / $7,000 (25K / 50K / 100K / 200K).

3. Inactivity

Zero requires one closed trade every 7 days. For Signature futures E8's pages disagree: one says one closed trade every 60 days, another says 7 days for all futures accounts. I can't resolve it, so I'd plan on 7 days. The rule applies to new accounts too, travel exceptions only if you write to support beforehand, and an account disabled for inactivity isn't refunded.

Example (illustrative): your last closed trade is day 0 and you go on holiday for 10 days with no message to support. Under the 7-day reading you've breached it on day 8; under the 60-day reading you're fine. With the downside being the account, I'd ask support in writing first.

Also: bots, AI tools and automated trading are banned on futures (including 300+ trades a day), at least half your profit must come from trades held 10 seconds or longer, and you must trade the front-month contract. E8's risk team can interview you "at any stage" and limit or withhold payouts, so the rules above are not the whole contract.

Payouts

  • Frequency: Zero: daily. Signature: on demand once the rules are met; the earliest first payout is about 3 days into the funded stage. Payouts only happen in the Performance stage.
  • Minimum: $100 net of the split. At 80% you must request at least $125.
  • Caps: per request, by size, as above. Signature caps for payouts 1–2 (bought after 1 Oct 2026): $1,000 / $1,250 / $2,000 / $3,000 (25K / 50K / 100K / 150K); payouts 3–4: $1,250 / $2,000 / $3,000 / $3,500.
  • Payouts per account: 4 for Signature and Zero accounts bought after 1 October 2026. After the last payout the cycle closes, the account is deactivated and you get a free challenge of the same size.
  • Speed: E8's own medians (January to May 2026, all products): 11 hours to approval, 24 hours to money; "up to 5 business days", no weekend processing.
  • Methods: WorkMarket, Rise and Aeropay (US only), each with its own KYC. E8 charges no payout fee; a processor may.

I have no payout timings of my own for E8. See our payout speed guide.

FAQ

Does E8 Markets actually pay out?

I don't know first-hand: I haven't traded E8 and it hasn't paid me. E8 says its median time from request to money was 24 hours (January to May 2026), which is its own figure. Some Trustpilot reviewers say caps and buffers cut what they can take out (unverified), and Trustpilot shows no score. Payouts are capped and accounts retire after 4 or 5, so ask how much you can take out.

What time must I be flat on E8 futures?

E8's futures help center says positions are force-closed at 15:10 CT, with trading from 17:15 to 15:10 CT, and no overnight or weekend holding on Zero or Signature. A shared Signature article mentions 23:00 server time; check the futures help center.

How many payouts can an E8 futures account make?

E8's pages disagree by purchase date: 4 for accounts bought after 1 October 2026, then a free challenge of the same size, and 5 for older accounts. Check which applies at checkout.

Is E8's futures drawdown trailing?

It is end-of-day dynamic, not intraday trailing: the loss line moves once a day at the close. But the account is breached if equity or balance reaches the line at any moment, including an open floating loss.

Can I use a trading bot at E8?

Not on futures. E8 bans semi-automated or fully automated trading, including bots and AI tools. Its CFD products have different rules.
