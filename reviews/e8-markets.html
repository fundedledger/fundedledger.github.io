My experience with E8 Markets

I haven't traded E8 Markets, and it hasn't paid me. Everything on this page comes from public reviews and Trustpilot, read in October 2026. I have no pass, no payout certificate and no experience of its support to show you.

What I can tell you is what I look for. On futures I only buy end-of-day trailing drawdown, after intraday trailing cost me Apex accounts again and again. E8's futures accounts are reported to use end-of-day drawdown, which is why I looked at it. On CFD firms I only trade static drawdown; one E8 forex product, E8 Pro, is reported to have a static one. That's my own rule, not an endorsement.

For comparison, Lucid Trading has paid me, and I can say so. E8 has not.

Watch out: hidden score and payout caps

Watch out: Trustpilot shows no score for E8 Markets. Its page says the rating is unavailable "due to a breach of our guidelines", and the page notes some reviews were removed as fake. The page lists 3,302 reviews, mostly 5-star, but with no official score I won't quote a rating for E8, and other sites' scores differ. Treat any E8 rating you see with caution.
  • Payout complaints, unverified. Trustpilot reviewers in October 2026 describe repeated payout denials, and profit caps and a 50% buffer limiting what they could withdraw. Trustpilot doesn't verify these claims. Some of this may be explained by the buffer rules below, but I can't tell which.
  • July 2026 futures rule change. One reviewer reports that from about 14 July 2026, new Signature and Zero futures accounts have per-request payout caps and a limit of 5 payouts per account, after which you get a free challenge of the same size. Only one source reports this. Caps per request apply, check E8 for the current figures.
  • E8 Zero is new. It launched in July 2026, so it has a short track record.
  • No licence. Reviewers report E8 is a simulated-trading service, not a regulated broker. I found no regulator warning, but I didn't search every regulator list.
  • Bots are banned on futures. That includes AI and semi-automated execution. Reviewers say bots are allowed on forex, but identical EAs across users can trigger action.
  • Coupon-site codes. A Trustpilot reviewer who says they are an affiliate claims a payout was refused after support found their discount code on a coupon site. That is unverified, but use any code only through the link on this page, never from a coupon site.

Payouts

  • Split: 80% by default; reviewers report 90% or 100% as a paid add-on on some products.
  • Frequency: varies by product. Reviewers report on-demand requests on E8 One and Signature, and daily requests on Pro and Zero.
  • Minimums: $100 net on the futures products, per one reviewer.
  • Buffers: Signature reportedly requires you to leave an amount equal to the drawdown in the account permanently. Pro reportedly lets you request 50% of profit and keeps the other 50% as a buffer.
  • Caps: caps per request apply, check E8 for the current figures. Together with the 5-payout limit on newer futures accounts, they limit how much of a big win you can take out at once.
  • Speed: one reviewer says E8 reports a median of 11 hours to approval and 24 hours to funds (January to May 2026). That is E8's own claim, relayed second-hand, and I haven't verified it.
  • Methods: reported as Rise, WorkMarket and Aeropay, depending on region.
