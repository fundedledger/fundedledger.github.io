Tradesyncer Review (2026)

A cloud trade copier for futures prop accounts, from $49 a month with a 7-day trial that needs a card. It's still labelled an open beta, payments are non-refundable, and your prop firm's own rule decides whether you may use it at all.

Updated November 2026Prices and rules checked November 2026I've used it as a trade copier

Disclosure: this review contains an affiliate link. If you buy through it I may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. There is no discount code.

Verdict: Tradesyncer is worth a look if you run several futures accounts that you own yourself and each firm allows copying. It connects to Tradovate, Rithmic, ProjectX (TopstepX) and Volumetrica/dxFeed accounts, and the company says copying runs in the cloud, so you don't need a VPS. Plans start at $49 a month. Before you pay: it calls itself an open beta, its terms cap liability at one month's fee and make payments non-refundable, and Trustpilot's 1-star reviews mention missed copies, disconnects and charges after the trial. A copier fault on a funded account is your loss. Check your firm's rule first (table below). If you start the trial, cancel before day 7 unless you want to pay, and screenshot the cancellation.

  • Free trial: 7 days, card required, charged on day 7. One trial per user and payment method. It converts to a paid plan automatically unless you cancel before it ends.
  • Refunds: the terms say payments are "final and non-refundable for the current billing period". It may issue a discretionary refund in exceptional cases, with a 20% administrative fee. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the billing period, with no prorated refund.

  • Features (the company's claims): per-follower multiplier including mini-to-micro mapping, bracket orders (take profit and stop loss) that copy and update, a mode that copies only leader orders that actually fill, unlimited copy groups, and risk settings such as daily loss limits, profit triggers and session lockouts, enforced in the cloud. It also says average copy speed is 20-40ms and that execution depends on your broker, connection and market conditions. I haven't measured any of this.
  • Own accounts only: its terms prohibit managing accounts owned by anyone else and reverse trading. Its firm page says to confirm that each firm allows third-party copiers.

Watch-outs

  • Open beta. The terms say it's "currently offered as an open beta in an advanced stage", and that it doesn't guarantee continuous availability or full compatibility with third-party platforms.
  • Liability is capped, and you carry the trades. Its liability is limited to a single month's subscription fee, and you are "solely responsible for all trades executed through the trade copier". If a copy fails on a funded account, the loss is yours.
  • Non-refundable. See the plans section. The trial needs a card and converts on day 7.
  • Trustpilot complaints (third party, individual claims). Trustpilot showed 4.5 from 721 reviews on 3 November 2026, with 12% at 1 star. I read the first page of 1-star reviews (20 reviews, June to October 2026). Themes: trades not copying or copying unevenly, charges after the trial when a cancellation wasn't registered, disconnects, and slow support. Most recent reviews (late October to early November) are 4 or 5 stars. These are unverified claims, and most of the 1-star reviews I read had no company reply. I didn't read the other pages of 1-star reviews.
  • Copier faults can look like hedging. One reviewer claims a firm closed accounts for counter-trading after opposite-direction trades. That's unverified, but the risk is real in principle: if a copy goes wrong, your accounts can show opposite positions.
  • Hedging rules apply to copiers too. Firms such as Alpha Futures and Topstep ban cross-account hedging and similar conduct, whether you place the trades or a copier does.
  • Rules change. A firm that allows copiers today can change its page. Re-check before you add accounts.
  • Speed and "built with the firms" are the vendor's claims. I haven't verified them.

Test it on demo or small accounts first and check every fill before you copy to a funded account.

Who Tradesyncer is for

A good fit: futures traders with several of their own accounts at firms that allow copiers, who'd rather not run a VPS and are willing to test on small accounts first.

Not a good fit: anyone at a firm whose rule you haven't confirmed, anyone who wants a guaranteed refund, or a trader who can't afford a missed or duplicated copy on a funded account. A firm's free native copier, where one exists, may be enough; see the prop trader toolkit.

FAQ

Is the Tradesyncer free trial really free?

It's a 7-day trial, but a card is required and you're charged on day 7 unless you cancel before then. Cancel under Subscription in your profile and screenshot the confirmation.

Does Tradesyncer work with MetaTrader or TradeLocker?

I found no mention of MetaTrader, cTrader or TradeLocker on the pages I read. It lists Tradovate, Rithmic, ProjectX (TopstepX) and Volumetrica/dxFeed, and has a form to request an integration.

Do prop firms allow Tradesyncer?

It depends on the firm and the plan. Lucid, FundedNext Futures and Alpha Futures publish rules that allow copying under conditions (see the table). Apex, Tradeify, Take Profit Trader and E8 are unknown to me: check the firm's help centre or ask support.

Does Topstep allow a cloud copier like Tradesyncer?

I can't tell you. Topstep has its own native copier and restricts VPS and remote servers; it doesn't clearly say how a cloud copier is treated. Ask Topstep support in writing.

Is there a Tradesyncer discount code?

No. I don't have a code and I won't make one up. Check the price at checkout.
