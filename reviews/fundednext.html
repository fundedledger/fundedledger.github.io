My experience with FundedNext

I've bought FundedNext evaluations, but FundedNext hasn't paid me out yet. So this review is based on its published rules and my time in its evaluations, not on a payout I've received. Weigh it against the firms that have paid me: FTMO, Lucid Trading and Crypto Fund Trader.

On CFD firms I only trade static drawdown, and every Stellar plan uses it. That's the main reason FundedNext is on my shortlist at all.

Which plan to pick: the 2-Step

The 2-Step has the biggest static drawdown (10%), the most daily room (5%), a lower first target than FTMO (8% vs 10%), and the fee comes back with your first payout. The 1-Step costs more and leaves only 6% of drawdown and 3% a day. Lite is cheaper, but you give up 2% of drawdown and wait three payouts for the refund.

If you're choosing between FundedNext's 2-Step and FTMO's, see FTMO vs FundedNext.

Payouts and the 24-hour claim

FundedNext says nearly all payouts (it claims 99.99%) are processed within 24 hours, and it has advertised a written 24-hour guarantee with compensation if it misses. That's a strong promise, but it's the firm's own claim. Two things to weigh against it:

  • Public payout reporting stopped. One payout-proof tracker noted FundedNext's monthly payout report stopped after February 2026. That doesn't mean it stopped paying, but there's less public evidence than there used to be.
  • Timing differs by plan. On the 2-Step, the first payout comes after 21 days and then every 14 days, slower than FTMO's day-14 first payout.

It hasn't paid me yet, so I can't confirm the 24-hour claim myself. If you buy, a small first account and an early payout is the cheapest way to check it.

Who FundedNext is for

A good fit: forex and gold traders who want an easier, cheaper 2-Step with static drawdown, and are willing to test payouts with a smaller account first.

Not a good fit: traders who want the most proven payout record. FTMO has paid me $26,138 in 2026.
