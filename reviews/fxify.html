My experience with FXIFY

I haven't traded FXIFY, and it hasn't paid me. Everything on this page comes from FXIFY's own website and blog and from public reviews, all checked in October 2026. FXIFY's individual plan pages and help center could not be read when I researched this, so many rules below come from FXIFY's blog posts or from third parties, and I say which. I have no pass, no payout certificate and no experience of its support to show you.

What I can share is a rule I follow: on CFD firms I only trade static drawdown. That is a rule of mine, not experience of FXIFY. On FXIFY's plan list it would point to Classic, Pro or Three Phase, not One Phase or Standard.

For comparison, FTMO and Crypto Fund Trader have paid me, and I can say so. FXIFY has not.

Watch out: payout denials and plan rules

Watch out: Trustpilot 1-star reviews describe payouts forfeited after "latency arbitrage" accusations with no trade-level evidence. FXIFY says it can't disclose its detection methods. A competing reviewer (MyForexFirms, 26 July 2026) cites a $4,310 denial, an EA flagged at withdrawal and a trader removed from Discord after questioning a denial. I can't verify any of this; treat it as allegations, but it is the main reason to test with a small account and an early first payout.
  • KYC and payout delays. Trustpilot reviewers report payouts stuck in review for about 3 business days and proof-of-address delays. Per QuantVPS (10 October 2026), the help center says payouts take 2 to 3 business days and the first one needs agreements signed through Riseworks.
  • Add-ons cost extra. The headline "up to 90%" split and bi-weekly payouts are paid add-ons at checkout. The base split is 80%.
  • Trailing on several plans. FXIFY says trailing max loss follows your highest closed balance, but the daily loss limit is checked on real-time equity. One Phase has only 6% max loss. See the table.
  • Inactivity. Per QuantVPS, no trade in 60 days breaches the account on all plans.
  • Banned practices. Per QuantVPS: HFT, reverse or group hedging, account management and latency arbitrage lead to termination and forfeited payouts. Copying third-party traders is prohibited. EAs are not allowed on Instant Funding or Lightning.
  • Simulated accounts. CryptoSlate (a third party) says all accounts are simulated and the liquidity provider is not named. MyForexFirms names FXPIG as the broker backing. Sources differ, and I couldn't confirm which is right.
  • Plans change often. FXIFY launched Two Phase Pro on 29 April 2026 and the two-phase line is now split into Standard, Classic and Pro. Treat any page, including this one, as possibly stale.

Payouts

  • Split: 80% base on the CFD plans, up to 90% as a paid add-on (FXIFY homepage; base split per QuantVPS). Two Phase Pro is 80% with no add-on. Sources differ on whether Classic goes higher; I'd treat 90% as the ceiling.
  • First payout: on demand on One, Two and Three Phase, as early as your first funded trading day, with a $50 minimum (FXIFY homepage).
  • Cycles (QuantVPS, 10 October 2026): One Phase, Standard and Three Phase every 30 days after the first (a 14-day add-on exists); Classic every 14 or 30 days; Pro and Instant Lite every 10 days; Instant Funding first at 14 days after your first trade, then every 14 days; Lightning first at 7 days, then every 14 days.
  • Review time: per QuantVPS, requests are reviewed Monday to Friday, 9am to 9pm GMT. Treat the 2 to 3 business days as the minimum, and see the denial complaints above.

FAQ

Does FXIFY actually pay out?

I don't know first-hand: I haven't traded it and it hasn't paid me. Trustpilot shows 4.3 from 6,539 reviews, mostly positive, but some 1-star reviews allege payouts denied for latency arbitrage. Those are unverified allegations, so test with a small account and an early payout.

Is FXIFY's drawdown static or trailing?

It depends on the plan. Per FXIFY's blog, Classic, Pro and Three Phase are static; One Phase, Standard, Instant Funding and Lightning are trailing on your highest closed balance. Daily loss is checked on real-time equity on all of them.

Is there an FXIFY discount code?

I don't have one of my own, so I'm not listing one. FXIFY runs frequent public sales that expire within days, so check the checkout for whatever sale is live when you buy.

Does FXIFY refund the fee?

FXIFY's homepage says the fee is reimbursed with your first payout on One, Two and Three Phase. Per QuantVPS, Two Phase Pro fees are not refundable. The refund comes only if you reach a payout.

Can I use an EA or trade news on FXIFY?

Per QuantVPS (10 October 2026), EAs and news trading are allowed on One, Two and Three Phase, though FXIFY warns of poor fills around news. They aren't allowed on Instant Funding and Lightning, which also have a news blackout. Some reviewers report EAs flagged at withdrawal, so check the current rules first.
