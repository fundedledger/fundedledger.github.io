Markets and data

  • Data: the vendor says historical tick data is included with no separate data subscription. Its own sources disagree on the symbol count (270+ on a features page, 200+ on its blog), so I'm leaving the number out.
  • Futures: the vendor says official CME tick data goes back to 2013 (it varies by symbol), with continuous contracts and rollover handling. Which plan includes futures: unverified.
  • Markets: the vendor lists forex, stocks, crypto, indices and futures. How deep each market is: unknown.
  • Features: order simulation, statistics and charts are mentioned. Order types and any trade journal: unverified. A company reply on Trustpilot (July 2026) says custom 3H, 6H and 12H timeframes were added.
  • Prop firm rules: the vendor has a page about passing prop firm challenges, but I couldn't read it, so I can't say whether it simulates a firm's profit target or drawdown rules. One Trustpilot reviewer (December 2025) praised its stress testing and blind testing. Whichever tool you use, track your firm's drawdown floor yourself (see the prop trader toolkit).

Watch-outs

These are customer opinions from Trustpilot unless noted. I can't verify them, and the company replies to negative reviews.

  • Trustpilot score. 3.9 from 115 reviews, 14 of them in the last 12 months (third party, read 3 November 2026). 77% are 5-star and 14% are 1 or 2-star. GodFunded showed 4.1 from 114 at another time.
  • Chart bugs. A February 2026 review describes the chart jumping back to 2003 on a timeframe change, freezing, and a crosshair offset with drawing tools. The company said the bugs were identified.
  • Drawings, indicators and projects. October 2025 reviews report drawings and indicators becoming unresponsive or removed, projects not reopening, a forward-testing bug, and "web version not worth it".
  • Data not downloading. A 1-star review dated 3 November 2026 says data hadn't finished downloading and the reviewer couldn't backtest.
  • Slow web version, no desktop. Reviewers in October and November 2025 called the web version slow and said the desktop version is gone.
  • Lifetime billing confusion. A January 2026 reviewer saw "subscription expired" on a lifetime purchase. The company said the payment had failed and that lifetime buyers get short-term access before payment confirms.
  • Custom indicators. A third-party summary (GodFunded) says custom indicators can't be used or converted.
  • Feature gaps after purchase. Reported in a March 2026 review. A refund-via-bank issue in October 2025 was resolved by the company.
  • Promo-heavy pricing. The price you see depends on the promotion, and coupon sites list unverified discounts.
  • Promotional comparison blogs. The vendor's own comparison posts (for example against FX Replay) are marketing; I treat their claims as claims.
