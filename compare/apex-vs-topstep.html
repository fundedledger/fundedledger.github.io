Apex vs Topstep (2026)

The two biggest names in futures funding, built very differently. Apex makes passing easy and payouts harder. Topstep charges you monthly but gives you more time and a gentler loss limit.

Updated October 2026I've bought evaluations at both

Short answer: pick Apex if you can pass fast and want a one-time fee. Choose its end-of-day drawdown, never intraday. Pick Topstep if you're newer, want no deadline, and would rather pay monthly than race a 30-day clock.

My experience

I've bought evaluations at both firms, and neither has paid me out yet. My biggest lesson came from Apex: intraday trailing drawdown cost me accounts again and again, so on futures I now only buy end-of-day trailing. Even end-of-day has ended funded accounts of mine that were in profit, but it's the best of the options futures firms offer.

Where Apex wins

  • Passing is easier. No consistency rule and no minimum days, so one strong session can finish the evaluation.
  • One-time fee. No monthly bill that grows the longer it takes.
  • You choose your drawdown. The end-of-day option is the right one, but you have to remember to pick it.

Where Topstep wins

  • No deadline. Apex gives you 30 days per fee; Topstep lets you take your time.
  • No intraday trap. Topstep only offers end-of-day trailing, so you can't pick the option that cost me Apex accounts.
  • Track record. Topstep has been around since 2012, longer than almost any futures firm.

The rule that decides it for most traders

Both firms have a consistency rule; it just bites at different times. Topstep's ~55% best-day rule applies while you're trying to pass, so one big day raises your target. Apex's 50% rule applies before each payout, so one big day locks the payout button until your other days catch up. If you make your money in a few big days, both will slow you down; Apex just lets you get funded first.
