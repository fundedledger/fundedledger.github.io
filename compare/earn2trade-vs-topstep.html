Where Earn2Trade fits

  • Smaller entry size. A 25K tier exists, with a $1,750 target.
  • Fewer funded-stage rules. No minimum days and no consistency rule on LiveSim or Live; the help center says news trading isn't restricted.
  • Weekly payout schedule. The help center describes weekly processing.

Watch out (Earn2Trade): "end-of-day" drawdown still breaches on open equity in real time, and the hard daily loss limit fails the evaluation. Live accounts move to trailing drawdown. The split is 50% on withdrawals below the threshold. Funded-stage costs add up ($139 activation, data fees, withdrawal fees). Subscriptions renew and aren't refundable. Earn2Trade describes itself as an educational company, and funding comes from a separate firm I couldn't identify on the pages I read. Its own figure is that 8.89% of candidates passed in 2025, and only 5.23% of those traded Live. Trustpilot showed 4.5 from 5,046 reviews in late October, but 1-star themes include contract-limit failures and slow support (unverified). I couldn't find an official rule on copy trading or bots, and sources disagree: ask support.

Where Topstep fits

  • Flat 90/10 split. It doesn't depend on withdrawal size.
  • No mandatory daily loss limit. It's an optional add-on.
  • Long-established name. Over a decade in business; sources differ on the exact year.

Watch out (Topstep): I couldn't verify the Combine price from an official page, and monthly billing means a slow pass costs more. The 55%-of-target consistency rule applies while you're trying to pass. Payout requests are capped at 50% of balance up to a fixed amount on the Standard path. The split and caps changed during 2026 (the policy page mentions traders who joined the new dashboard before 12 January 2026 having different terms), and a limited-time offer to raise caps has no announced end date, so check the policy page. Passing closes the Combine, and profits don't transfer to the funded account. Only 16.8% of Combines complete (Topstep's own 2025 figure). There is no 25K size. The difference that decides it for most traders

Both firms use a monthly subscription and a $3,000 target on a 50K-class account, so the evaluation stage is close on paper. The gap opens once you're funded. Topstep's 90% split is predictable, but each request is capped. Earn2Trade lets a larger withdrawal earn 80% instead of 50%, but you pay the activation fee and the Live account moves to trailing drawdown. Work out your likely withdrawal size before you compare.
