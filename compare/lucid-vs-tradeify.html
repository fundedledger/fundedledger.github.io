Lucid Trading vs Tradeify (2026)

Two fast-growing futures firms with nearly identical headline rules: end-of-day drawdown and a 90/10 split. The differences are in price, daily loss limits and how much you can withdraw at once.

Updated October 2026I've traded both; Lucid has paid me

Short answer: Lucid is usually cheaper, has no daily loss limit on its Flex and Pro plans, and pays fast; mine was instant. Tradeify lets you withdraw more per payout, but its Growth plan adds a daily loss limit and a consistency rule. For most traders I'd start with Lucid.

My experience

I've bought evaluations at both. Lucid Trading is one of the three firms that have paid me, and that payout was instant, the fastest I've had from any firm. Tradeify hasn't paid me out yet.

Both firms use end-of-day trailing drawdown, the only kind I'll buy on futures now.

Where Lucid wins

  • No daily loss limit on Flex and Pro, so one bad morning doesn't end your day.
  • Cheaper at most account sizes.
  • Fast payouts. Mine arrived instantly.

Where Tradeify wins

  • Bigger payouts per request, which matters once you're consistently profitable.
  • Size and track record. It reports more than $150M paid out.
  • A one-day pass is possible on the Growth plan.

Watch out for the plan names

Both firms sell several plans with different rules under one brand. A "Lucid" or "Tradeify" account can mean very different things. Before buying, check four things on the exact plan: drawdown type, daily loss limit, consistency rule and payout cap.
