Where the sources disagree

Sources differ on:
  • Starting prices. Aggregator sites list different “from” prices for each firm and don't agree with each other: one pairing shows $19 and $33, another $59 and $32 (propfirmmap.com/vs/fxify-vs-fundednext and others, noted 3 November 2026). I haven't re-verified those figures and I don't use them. The prices on this page are from the firms' own pages.
  • FXIFY prices. FXIFY's program pages show one generic “$250 for a $5k account” card and its homepage says “as little as $39”. Only Two Phase Pro has an official price list.
  • FXIFY first-payout timing. Program pages say the first payout is on demand for all plans. The Two Phase Pro blog table (29 April 2026) says on-demand only for Standard; Classic and Pro are “based on payout cycle” (Pro: every 10 days).
  • FXIFY minimum trading days and split. The homepage says no minimum days and up to 100%; program pages say 5 days and up to 90%.
  • FundedNext payout speed. Help articles say requests are processed within 24 hours of approval. The homepage also shows a 40-hour average and a “99.99% within 24 hours” claim.
  • FundedNext “up to 95%”. It appears in the homepage metadata and on Trustpilot's profile, but it is the paid Lifetime add-on. The default is 80%, or 90% on On-Demand.

Drawdown: static or trailing

This is the rule that ends most accounts, so check the exact program before you buy.

  • FundedNext: every Stellar challenge measures its limits from the initial balance (max loss 10% on 2-Step, 6% on 1-Step, 8% on Lite). The wording says balance or equity must not fall below the floor, so an open trade floating into the red can breach you. Stellar Instant is the exception, with a 6% trailing max loss.
  • FXIFY: it depends on the plan. Classic, Pro and Three Phase are static. Standard and One Phase are trailing (on Standard it follows your highest closed balance and locks at breakeven). Instant Funding and Lightning trail too. Daily loss is judged on real-time equity.

On CFD firms I only trade static drawdown, so on FXIFY I'd look only at Classic, Pro or Three Phase. Trailing vs end-of-day drawdown explains the types.

  • Lower first targets on Pro (4% then 8%) and on Classic's first phase (5%).
  • First payout on demand on Standard, per the Pro blog (program pages say all plans), after the first closed live trade, $50 minimum.
  • Replies to negative Trustpilot reviews (97%), though a reply isn't a fix.

Watch out (FXIFY):

  • Payout denials. 1-star reviews cite payouts cancelled or forfeited under latency-arbitrage or prohibited-trading rules, with no evidence shown. If you scalp very fast, read its prohibited-strategies rules first.
  • Trailing vs static depends on the plan. Standard and One Phase trail. A wrong plan choice changes the whole account.
  • Daily loss is on live equity, so floating losses count.
  • US residents are restricted.
  • Pro has a $4,000 daily profit cap and a day counts for minimum days only if it closes at least +0.5%.
  • Rules sit in blogs and FAQs that disagree on minimum days, split and caps. Screenshot what applies when you buy.

Where FundedNext wins

  • Static limits on every Stellar challenge, the only type I trade on CFD firms.
  • A first target of 8% on the 2-Step, with a 5% daily limit and a 10% max loss.
  • A payout schedule you choose (80%, 60% or 90% options on the 2-Step), and a far larger Trustpilot base.

Watch out (FundedNext):

  • Stellar rules that end accounts fast. The Quick Strike rule limits trades closed in under 30 seconds (2-Step), a news rule counts only 40% of funded-account profit from trades within 5 minutes of high-impact news, and EAs aren't allowed on Stellar at $50K and above.   • The payout option can't be changed after checkout on the 2-Step.
  • Rules change often. The Lite cycle changed on 12 October 2026 and the refund timing on 12 January 2026.
