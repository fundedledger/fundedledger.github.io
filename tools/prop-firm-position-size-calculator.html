How the math works

  1. Risk per contract = stop in points × point value. A 20-point stop on MNQ ($2 per point) is $40 per contract.
  2. Contracts = risk ÷ risk per contract, rounded down. $200 ÷ $40 = 5 contracts.
  3. If your firm caps contracts, the calculator uses the lower of the two numbers.
  4. Commissions and slippage are not included. A fast market can fill your stop worse than planned, so leave some margin.
