How the calculation works

  1. Expected attempts = 1 ÷ pass rate. At 33% that's about 3 attempts. At 20% it's 5.
  2. First attempt costs the full price (times the months you expect it to take, on monthly plans).
  3. Each retry costs the reset fee where the firm sells one and we have a source, otherwise a full re-buy.
  4. Activation fee, if any, is added once.

It's an average, not a promise: you could pass first time or fail ten times. It does not include market data, platform or commission costs, payout fees, taxes, or any refund a firm may give back after payouts. Pass rate is also a guess about you. It depends on your edge and on how well the firm's drawdown and consistency rules fit how you trade. For more on that, see the cheapest challenges guide and trailing vs end-of-day drawdown.

Why I built this

I've bought 50+ evaluations and passed 15+, roughly one in three. That's why the default is 33%. Many traders do worse than that, and the cheapest sticker price is often not the cheapest way to get funded. A firm with rules you can't stick to costs more than a pricier one you can.
